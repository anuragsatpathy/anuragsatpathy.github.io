I am a Post Doctoral Fellow working with <a href="https://sites.google.com/a/mst.edu/sdas/">Dr. Sajal K. Das</a> at Missouri University of Science and Technology, Rolla, Missouri, USA.

Prior to this, I completed my Ph.D. in February 2023 from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Rourkela, India, under the supervision of <a href="https://website.nitrkl.ac.in/FProfile.aspx?e=sahoom"> Dr. Manmath Narayan Sahoo </a>. My thesis, "Efficient Re-Embedding Strategies for Virtual Data Centers over Multi-Domain Substrate Networks," focused on developing effective resource management schemes to deal with fluctuating resource demands of virtual networks.


## Research Interests
My current research focuses on **behavior-aware and equitable decision-making for Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) under uncertainty**. I develop optimization, matching, and game-theoretic frameworks that integrate human behavior, fairness, and real-time constraints into large-scale CPS.

<section class="research-thread">
  <h2>Signature Research Direction</h2>
  <p>
    My recent work studies how shared CPS can recover when agents do not follow the original plan, resources become unavailable, or system priorities change. This line connects ReACT-TTC, W-TTC, and KATCH into a broader agenda for resilient resource exchange.
  </p>
  <div class="thread-flow">
    <div><strong>Shared CPS disruption</strong><span>non-compliance, delay, capacity shift</span></div>
    <div><strong>ReACT-TTC</strong><span>capacity-aware post-choice reassignment</span></div>
    <div><strong>W-TTC</strong><span>weighted utility-aware exchange</span></div>
    <div><strong>KATCH</strong><span>capacity and open-slot aware reassignment</span></div>
  </div>
</section>
