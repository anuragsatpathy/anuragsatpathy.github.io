My service interests include professional community building, peer review, student mentoring, research translation, and outreach around Cyber-Physical Systems, intelligent transportation, and public safety technologies.

## Research and Professional Service

- Technical lead contributions in the NSF I-Corps ecosystem, including Local, Regional, and BOOST activities.
- Contributions to NSF proposal development in EV charging, energy-aware coordination, public safety systems, and broader impacts.
- Peer-review and scholarly service activities in computer networks, distributed systems, Cyber-Physical Systems, cloud-edge computing, and IoT.

## Mentoring and Outreach

I have mentored Ph.D., master's, and undergraduate students on projects spanning CPS, vehicular edge computing, cloud-edge systems, virtual machine placement, matching theory, and optimization. My mentoring style emphasizes clear problem formulation, reproducible evaluation, technical writing, and independent research judgment.

## Service Interests

- Program committee and reviewer service in CPS, networking, distributed systems, and IoT venues
- Student mentoring and research training
- Interdisciplinary collaborations with transportation, healthcare, energy, and emergency-management communities
- Outreach that connects computing research with societal-scale infrastructure challenges
